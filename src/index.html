Questions.

When and where is ForgeHacks?

February 26–28, 2027 at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver, BC, with 26 hours of building.

Who is ForgeHacks for?

Students across engineering, computer science and commerce, including UBC design team members and FIRST/VEX robotics alumni. Teams combine hardware, software and design skills.

What will teams build?

Working physical prototypes. Projects can span robotics, autonomous systems, consumer hardware and sustainability, using sensors, microcontrollers and fabrication tools.

Can we contribute hardware or expertise?

Yes. We welcome hardware, tools, fabrication credits and technical workshops alongside monetary sponsorship. Contact Stella to discuss your team’s involvement.
